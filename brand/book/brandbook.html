Brand book · version 1

connectly

A verified community that helps you get matched.

Working name. Trademark clearance has not been done.

The idea

A doorway, not a catalogue.

What we are

A community where verified people meet. Not a photo wall. A man writes a note and sends a request. A woman reads it and chooses: come in, or not now.

What we believe

Trust is the product. Everyone is a real person. Women decide who gets in. Nobody is ranked by a photo.

How we sound

Warm, plain and calm. We say "we help you get matched". We do not say swipe, hot or hookup.

Space and size

Give it room.

Clear space

Keep the width of the door opening free on every side. Nothing else enters this area: no text, edges or other marks.

Minimum size

Symbol: 16 px on screen, 6 mm in print. Below 24 px use the small-size cut, which has a larger dot and opening. Horizontal lockup: 96 px wide.

Colour

Three colours. That is all.

Ink#10252B
RGB 16 37 43
Text, mark, dark surfaces
Ember#F9502C
RGB 249 80 44
The person: dot, accent, action
Paper#F6F1E9
RGB 246 241 233
Backgrounds, reversed marks

Ink does the work. Ember appears once per screen or less. Why this palette: dating apps already own coral gradients, yellow and near-black. Deep teal-ink with one warm ember reads as a lit door at night.

Typography

Poppins for voice, a clear sans for reading.

Aa

Poppins SemiBold

Headlines, numbers and the wordmark. The wordmark is outlined from this font (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

Aa

System sans

Body text and interface. Use Inter where it can be bundled, otherwise the device's own sans. Body 16 px or larger. Line height 1.4 to 1.5.

Connectly brand book · 8
Voice

Say it like a good host.

We say

"We help you get matched."
"Not now" (never "reject").
"Come in."
"A verified member reads every request."

We don't say

"Swipe", "hot", "hookup".
"Rejected" or "declined".
Anything that ranks people by looks.
Promises we can't keep.

Short sentences. Plain words. Honest about money: say how many requests a pack gives and what it costs, before the person pays.

In the product

Arches everywhere, quietly.

Women

Free. They choose who comes in.

Men

Pay per request. A real chance.

Rules of use

Arch-top shapes for people and steps. Rounded corners 18 to 28 px. One Ember action per screen, with Ink text. No profile photos anywhere: members show who they are in words.
