# Palette (shared by all three marks)

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#10252B` | Text, primary mark colour, dark surfaces |
| Ember | `#F9502C` | The "person": dot, accent, primary action |
| Paper | `#F6F1E9` | Backgrounds, reversed marks |

Why these: dating apps already own coral-pink gradients (Tinder), yellow (Bumble) and near-black (Hinge). Deep teal-ink with a single warm
ember accent reads as "a lit door at night": calm and trustworthy with one warm point of life.

## Contrast (WCAG 2.x)
| Foreground on background | Ratio | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink on Paper | 14.1:1 | Body text |
| Paper on Ink | 14.1:1 | Text on dark |
| Ember on Ink | 4.7:1 | Dot on dark, accents (large and small text OK) |
| Ink on Ember | 4.7:1 | Text on Ember buttons |
| Ember on Paper | 3.0:1 | Graphics and large UI only, never body text |
| Paper on Ember | 3.0:1 | Avoid for text: use Ink on Ember instead |

Rules: never put Paper text on Ember; Ember is never used for body-size text on Paper.
